Where the gaps are

1. Enterprise and complex sales

Confidence: evidenced
What this covers
Multi stakeholder deals, procurement, security reviews, long cycles.
The evidence this rests on
Holland & Barrett - approximately 100 stores in London supported with e-cargo bikes and inventory analytics software
Source: https://ridelogix.com/about-us
If this stays unfilled for six months
Without someone experienced in navigating enterprise procurement and multi-stakeholder buying committees, expansion within accounts like Holland & Barrett or replication with similar large retailers stalls, and deals get stuck in pilot purgatory.
Who fills it
Has closed six figure contracts into regulated or large organisations. Knows what a security questionnaire and a DPA do to a deal cycle.
Three questions for the first twenty minutes
  • Walk me through the last enterprise deal you lost, and at which stage
  • Who signs, and who can veto
  • What does your security review pack look like today

2. Positioning and product marketing

Confidence: evidenced
What this covers
Who it is for, what it replaces, why now. The words before the funnel.
The evidence this rests on
Next-generation inventory ecosystem for urban retail with sustainable, zero-emissions delivery
Source: https://ridelogix.com
If this stays unfilled for six months
Prospects and investors have to do the work of translating the pitch into 'why this, why now, for me' themselves, which slows qualification and weakens inbound conversion.
Who fills it
Has repositioned a company and can show the before and after. Works from customer interviews, not from a workshop.
Three questions for the first twenty minutes
  • Who is this not for
  • What do customers use instead of you today
  • What did the last ten closed won deals have in common

What this is, and what it is not. This report was produced by reading the company's own public website. Nobody has spoken to the team. It records what is publicly visible and, where it cannot see something, it says so rather than filling the gap with an assumption. Every gap named here carries the specific evidence it rests on and a confidence level, so any claim can be checked or dismissed in seconds.

A gap marked open question is not a finding. It means the evidence pointed that way but was not strong enough to stand behind, and the honest thing is to ask rather than assert.

Where it says we could not find something, that means exactly that. It is a statement about what your website shows us, not a claim about your company. If we have missed something that is there, the report is wrong and we would rather know.
