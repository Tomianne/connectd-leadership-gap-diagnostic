commercial-revenue-leader
Discarded. Contradicted by: Lili Kyuchuk-Pakyuz holds the title Co-Founder & COO. While COO is an operations title rather than a sales/revenue title, the presence of a named COO directly contradicts the claim that there is no commercial leadership. A COO at a company with an active paying customer (Holland & Barrett) is evidence of commercial operations ownership, even if not titled 'Chief Revenue Officer' or 'VP Sales'. The claim conflates 'no sales title' with 'no commercial leadership', which are distinct.
