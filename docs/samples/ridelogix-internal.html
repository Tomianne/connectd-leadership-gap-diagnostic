Claims we tested and discarded

A second model reviewed every candidate gap and tried to disprove it from the same evidence. These did not survive.

commercial-revenue-leader
Discarded. Contradicted by: Holland & Barrett - approximately 100 stores in London supported with e-cargo bikes and inventory analytics software. A named enterprise customer at scale is direct evidence that revenue generation and commercial execution is happening. This contradicts the claim that revenue generation is happening 'without a visible owner of that function.' The absence of a titled sales leader does not contradict the existence of commercial capability; it suggests founder-led or COO-led selling, which is a leadership gap only if the claim is specifically about 'who owns this', not 'is this happening'. However, the reasoning conflates two different claims: the capability exists (contradicted by evidence), but the ownership question remains open.
enterprise-sales-gtm
Discarded. Contradicted by: Holland & Barrett - approximately 100 stores in London supported with e-cargo bikes and inventory analytics software. A live multi-location enterprise customer is direct evidence that enterprise sales and GTM execution has occurred. The absence of a titled enterprise sales leader does not contradict the existence of enterprise selling capability; it indicates the capability is being delivered through other means (founder-led, COO-led, or operational execution). The claim 'no team member holds an enterprise sales title' is true but does not contradict 'enterprise sales is happening', it only contradicts 'there is a dedicated enterprise sales leader role'.
